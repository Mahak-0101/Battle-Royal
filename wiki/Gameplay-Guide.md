# 🎯 Gameplay Guide

## Combat Rhythm

1. Keep moving.
2. Track enemies with camera control.
3. Fire with intent, then reposition.
4. Use pickups to maintain pressure.

## Weapon Flow

- Equip weapon
- Shoot projectile
- Trigger muzzle flash
- Confirm impact VFX

## Survival Tips

- Never hold one angle for too long.
- Use movement to break enemy line-of-fire.
- Prioritize clear shots over panic spray.
- Stay aware of pickups and spacing.

## Practice Loop

`Move → Flick Aim → Fire → Relocate → Repeat`
